Batched Contextual Reinforcement (BCR) studies how large reasoning models can learn from batches of related problems without supervised fine-tuning. The project introduces an implicit information bottleneck through multi-problem batching and analyzes how model scale changes the ability to preserve accuracy at higher batch sizes while compressing reasoning trajectories.

The work connects reinforcement learning, efficient reasoning, and scaling behavior across 0.6B to 8B parameter models.
