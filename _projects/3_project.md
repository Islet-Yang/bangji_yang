MutAtlas builds a large-scale atlas of protein mutation effects with energy-guided modeling. The project sits at the intersection of protein structure, mutation analysis, and machine learning for biological discovery.

The goal is to provide broad coverage over PDB-scale structures while retaining mechanistic signals useful for understanding mutation impact.
