ConfRover models protein conformation and dynamics within a unified autoregressive generation framework. The project combines ideas from SE(3)-aware generative modeling and causal transformers to simulate conformational trajectories and dynamic modes.

This work was developed during my research internship at ByteDance Seed on AI drug design.
